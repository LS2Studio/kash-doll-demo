Arkeisha Knight  •  Born March 14, 1992  •  Detroit, Michigan

From Joy Road to the world

Detroit Raised Her

Born Arkeisha Antoinette Knight, Kash Doll grew up on Joy Road in Detroit. She worked as an exotic dancer to support her family, then quit to bet on herself. Her first Detroit gigs came in 2014. She sold mixtapes out of her trunk and built her following one freestyle at a time.

Her first mixtape, K.R.E.A.M. (Kash Rules Everything Around Me), dropped in early 2015, followed by Keisha vs. Kash Doll in 2016. Then came "For Everybody", and a major label deal. "Ice Me Out" went Gold and became a Detroit anthem.

Her debut album Stacked (2019) entered the Billboard 200 at No. 76. That same year she toured with Meek Mill and earned the Spirit of Detroit Award for her charitable work through Kash Doll Enterprises. In 2020 she stood beside Eminem and Royce Da 5'9" on Big Sean's "Friday Night Cypher." In 2023 she linked with DJ Drama for Back on Dexter: A Gangsta Grillz Mixtape, with Icewear Vezzo and Babyface Ray.

She is a mother of two, a BET Award nominee, and a multi-platinum artist who never left her city behind.

On screen  •  Starz

Monique

On Starz's BMF, Kash Doll plays Monique, a paralegal for the biggest defense attorney in Detroit and a strong single mom determined to give her daughters a better life. Monique is also one of Demetrius Flenory's lovers, and her firm's clients give Meech and Terry an edge in their rise through the streets.

She first auditioned for a different role, Keto. The producers called back and said Monique fit her better. She was right, and so were they. Monique ran recurring through seasons 1 and 2, and Kash Doll joined the main cast from season 4.

BMF is inspired by the true story of two brothers who rose from southwest Detroit in the late 1980s and built one of the most influential crime families in the country. It is executive produced by Curtis "50 Cent" Jackson.
